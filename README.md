## About

The Physics Research Council runs free physics competitions, open problem sets, and volunteer programmes for secondary-school and early undergraduate students. The aim is straightforward: give motivated students a clear path into advanced physics without fees or institutional barriers.

An international Scientific Advisory Committee of professors and researchers reviews materials and exam papers. Operations are handled by student volunteers.

---

## The Physics Championship 2026

A three-round competition testing conceptual understanding, portfolio work, and olympiad-level theory. Completely free to enter.

| Round | Format | Focus |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Round 1 | Online MCQ and numerical (60 min, 20 questions) | Conceptual filter: logic, dimensional analysis, core laws. 100 points. No negative marking. |
| Round 2 (optional) | Portfolio or CV submission | Research projects, simulations, literature reviews, or academic CV. Up to +25 bonus points. |
| Round 3 | Theory paper (3 hours, 5–8 problems) | Multi-stage derivation problems at international olympiad standard. 100 points. Graded by the Advisory Board. |

**Divisions**

- **Division A:** Classes 9 and 10  
- **Division B:** Classes 11–12 and gap year  
- **Division C:** College undergraduates (1st and 2nd year, any major)

**Syllabus topics (scaled by division)**  
Mechanics, Electromagnetism, Thermodynamics, Waves and Optics, Modern Physics.

**Awards**  
Gold / Silver / Bronze certificates (top 5% / next 10% / next 15% per division), Honorable Mention (top 50%), Hall of Fame listing, and letters of recommendation for the top 3 in each division.

## Student Volunteer Intake

Open to students from Class 9 through undergraduate level. No prior physics background is required for operational roles. Training is provided.

**Domains**

| Domain | Work |
|--------|------|
| Outreach and Email Communications | Schools, sponsors, advisors |
| Social Media Management and PR | Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Discord |
| Graphic Design and Content Creation | Flyers, posts, visual assets |
| Web Development and Portal Admin | HTML/CSS portal, databases |
| Event Operations and Logistics | Timelines, scheduling, Q&A boards |
| Physics Grading and Problem Design | Reviewing submissions and quizzes (suited to physics majors and olympiad alumni) |
| General Operational Support | Flexible help across teams |

**What volunteers receive**

- Certificate of Appreciation  
- Public listing as a contributor  
- Access to an academic community, ambitious peers, and the physics network in India  
- Occasional sponsor perks  

This is an unpaid volunteer role. There is no monetary stipend at present, though one may be introduced later.

## Scientific Advisory Committee

Professors, postdoctoral researchers, and educators who shape the syllabus, validate papers, and review student portfolios. Membership is honorary (roughly 2–4 hours per semester).

To join, email physicsresearchcouncil@gmail.com with a CV.
