# Round 3 Handbook (Problems 15–20)

## Problem 15: Waves and Optics (Thin Film Interference at Oblique Incidence)
Film index n, thickness d, incidence angle theta. Constructive interference (with top-surface phase shift):
$$2d\sqrt{n^2 - \sin^2\theta} = \left(m + \frac{1}{2}\right)\lambda$$

## Problem 16: Waves and Optics (Resolving Power of a Grating)
$$R = \frac{\lambda}{d\lambda} = m N$$

## Problem 17: Special Relativity (Relativistic Doppler Effect)
Source receding at v:
$$f = f_0\sqrt{\frac{1 - v/c}{1 + v/c}}$$

## Problem 18: Special Relativity (Threshold Energy for Pion Production)
$$K_{\text{th}} = 2 m_\pi c^2 \left(1 + \frac{m_\pi}{4m}\right)$$

## Problem 19: Modern Physics (Bohr-Sommerfeld Quantization of Harmonic Oscillator)
$$E = n \hbar \omega$$

## Problem 20: Modern Physics (Normalization of Infinite Well Wavefunction)
